# Community code of conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds. Treat people with respect, provide
constructive feedback, assume good faith and respect privacy. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing and non-consensual sharing of private media are not
acceptable. Keep discussions relevant and resolve technical disagreements with evidence.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect the
community. Report concerns privately to the repository owner; do not publicly share
private reports or personal information. Maintainers will consider context and impact
and communicate proportionate action.
