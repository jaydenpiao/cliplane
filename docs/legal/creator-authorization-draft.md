# Creator authorization — operational draft

Not a deployed legal contract. Adapt to jurisdiction, service and actual rights basis.

The uploading creator confirms they own or have authority to supply the recording,
authorizes its processing/editing, and authorizes use of their voice and likeness for
the requested clips. The persisted statement is versioned `creator-upload-v1` and linked
to the acting user, source checksum and workspace. Authorization does not transfer
rights in game assets, music, overlays or third-party works the creator cannot grant.

Commercial agency/revenue-share agreements should separately define approved accounts,
platforms, exclusivity, payment/revenue split, term, termination, removal process and
third-party rights responsibility. Do not contact creators or publishers automatically.

The review UI requires a recorded rights basis before export. This is an operator's
attestation; it is not automated legal advice. Embargoed/pre-release sources are locked.
