# Privacy, retention and takedown — operational draft

Not published legal terms. A public hosted operator must supply the actual entity,
contact details, processing regions, subprocessors, retention/backup practices and
jurisdiction-specific rights process before launch.

The local app stores account/session records, workspace membership, creator provenance,
source files, derivative media, reviews, workflow events and job telemetry. It sends no
media to a third-party AI provider and connects no social account. The optional Google
Fonts stylesheet requests fonts from Google; remove/self-host it for an offline deployment.

Original files and analysis artifacts expire after seven days by default. Approved
renders remain until source deletion. Deleting a source removes its derivatives and
retains a minimal audit record and workflow metadata. Account deletion and backup
cleanup require the follow-up issue in the backlog.

Before operating a U.S. hosted UGC service, establish applicable DMCA agent registration,
notice/takedown/counter-notice handling and a repeat-infringer policy. Provide a private
reporting channel, authenticate removal authority, preserve only the needed case record,
and process valid requests promptly. No legal request is generated or sent by this project.
